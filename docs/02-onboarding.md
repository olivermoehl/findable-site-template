# Onboarding questions

Ask these before writing anything. An agent asks them a few at a time and records the answers in `onboarding/brief.md`. Answers in the user's own words are worth more than polished ones; keep their wording where you can.

## Organisation

1. What is the legal name, and what name do customers use?
2. Where is the registered address? Which countries or regions do you serve?
3. Who founded or leads it, and what is that person's public profile (if any)?
4. In one sentence without adjectives: what do you do, and for whom?

## Buyers

5. Who decides to buy? Describe their role and the size and kind of organisation.
6. What situation are they in when they start looking? Describe three or four moments in their words.
7. What would they type into a search engine or ask an assistant at that moment? Collect at least ten phrases.
8. What do they fear or doubt about a solution like yours?

## Offer

9. What exactly do you deliver? What is the first step for a new customer?
10. What do you deliberately not do?
11. How does pricing work? If you do not publish prices, what drives the price?
12. Which technical terms do you use that a buyer may not know?

## Proof

13. Which results can you show? For each: the task, what you built, the measured change, how it was measured.
14. May you name the client? Is there written permission? If not, how may they be described?
15. Are there quotes you are allowed to publish, word for word?

## Trust and rules

16. Which legal or compliance topics matter to your buyers?
17. What must never appear on the site (topics, names, claims)?
18. Who reviews and approves changes?

## Brand

19. Brand colours (hex values) and typeface, or "none yet".
20. Logo file, if there is one.
21. How do you address readers (formal or informal)? Which language first?
22. Three words that describe the tone, and three you never want to sound like.

## Channels and measurement

23. Which company profiles exist elsewhere (LinkedIn, directories, registries)?
24. Do you have access to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for the domain?
25. Is a keyword data service available (see `docs/03-terms.md`), or should the first round use free sources only?
